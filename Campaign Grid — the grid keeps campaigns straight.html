Before you ask.

Is Campaign Grid available today?

Not yet. It is in development. Request early access and we’ll share progress as it takes shape.

What does version-locked approval mean?

An approval applies to one exact version. A later edit to budget or schedule creates a new version that needs its own review, while the approved one keeps running.

What happens when spend crosses the limit?

Each approved version has a cost-per-acquisition ceiling. When it is crossed, delivery pauses and the team is notified.

Which ad platforms will it connect to?

Connections to the major ad and messaging platforms are planned. The first set will be settled with early-access teams.
